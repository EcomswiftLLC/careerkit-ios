What you can do in CareerKit

Start from 30 resume templates
Six groups: Professional, Modern, Creative, Academic, Entry Level and Technical. Each template is a complete design with its own layout, fonts and heading style.
Or start from a blank page
Choose your own structure. Begin with a blank resume and add only the sections you need.
Change every detail
Add, hide and reorder sections. Change the accent colour, fonts, text size, line spacing and margins. Add a photo when the kind of application you are making expects one.
In your language
CareerKit is available in English, Spanish, French, German and Simplified Chinese. A resume can also be written in a different language from the app, and its headings and dates follow that language in every export.
Cover letters that match
Ten cover letter templates share the design of a matching resume, so the two documents look like a set.
Check the layout
The ATS check reads your resume and points out common formatting problems. It is general guidance and not a score from any employer system.
Keep several resumes
Keep one version for each kind of job. Duplicate, rename or archive them from My Resumes, and compare two versions side by side. Pick A4 or US Letter.
Export your way
Choose PDF, Word (.docx), Rich Text, plain text or a web page. Share it, save it to Files or print the PDF. The text in the PDF is selectable, and email, phone and web addresses are links.

30 templates on four page structures

Templates differ in more than colour. Each one sets its own header, heading style, date layout, bullet style and fonts. Underneath, every template uses one of four page structures.

  • Single column19 templates. One full-width column, the plainest structure and the easiest for software to read.

  • Left sidebar4 templates. A narrow tinted column on the left for contact details and skills.

  • Right sidebar3 templates. The same idea mirrored, with the main column reading first.

  • Header, then two columns4 templates. A full-width header over a main column and a narrow right rail.

Student, Entry level, Mid career, Senior and Executive.

Sixteen fonts that already ship with iOS, so nothing is downloaded: SF Pro, New York, Helvetica Neue, Avenir Next, Georgia, Palatino, Baskerville, Hoefler Text, Charter, Iowan Old Style, Gill Sans, Optima, Cochin, Didot, Futura and Menlo.
Plain by design
Some templates leave out sidebars, photos and decoration on purpose, because simple layouts are usually easier for software to read.

An ATS check that shows what it looked at

Many employers use an applicant tracking system, or ATS, to store and search applications. Systems differ a lot, and nobody outside them can promise how a resume will be read. CareerKit checks the things that commonly cause trouble and explains each finding.

  • Standard section headings
  • Email and phone details that look valid
  • Dates on every job
  • A summary at the top
  • Whether the text can be read back out of the PDF
  • Sidebars and multi-column layouts
  • A photo on the page
  • Unusual symbols
  • Length

Treat the result as a checklist. It does not score your resume, it cannot predict any employer's system, and a clean result is not a promise of anything.

Free to start, with optional exports

Writing, editing, previewing, cover letters and the ATS check are free and never need an ad or a purchase. Saving, sharing or printing a finished document uses one export, and there are three ways to get one. There is no subscription.

Ways to export

  • Watch one short rewarded video, if you choose to. One video covers one export of that document.
  • Buy a pack of 3 exports, a one-time purchase. Each file you save, share or print uses one.
  • Buy Premium for unlimited exports. It is a single purchase, and Restore purchases brings it back on a new device.

The App Store shows the price before you buy. Used-up packs cannot be restored.

Where you will not see ads

  • In the editor or the preview.
  • While you create, edit or preview a document, write a cover letter or run the ATS check.
  • As a full-screen ad that pops up, or when you open the app.

Ads from Google AdMob also support the app. A banner can appear on browse screens: Home, My Resumes, Templates, Cover Letters and Profile. Premium does not remove banners.

Where the law asks for it, the app shows a consent form before personalised ads, and iOS may ask whether the app may track you. You can say no to the iOS tracking prompt and still use everything. If you decline a consent form that applies in your region, ads may not load. The app tells you when no video is available, and you can still export with a pack or Premium. You can change that choice later in the Profile tab. The privacy policy explains what the ad network collects.

Your documents stay on your device

CareerKit has no account and no server that stores your resumes. What you write is saved on your device and is not uploaded. The only outside service is the ad network. There is no AI service behind the app, so your work is never sent to one.

You can delete everything from the Profile tab whenever you like. The app can save a backup file first.

Questions

Is CareerKit free?

CareerKit is free to download. Writing, editing, previewing, cover letters and the ATS check are free, with no limit. Saving, sharing or printing a finished document uses one export. You can watch one short rewarded video for one export, buy a pack of 3 exports, or buy Premium for unlimited exports. Premium is a single purchase and not a subscription, and the App Store shows the price before you buy. Ads from Google AdMob also support the app: a banner on browse screens and the video you choose to watch.

How do exports work?

One completed video covers one export of that document, and that export stays unlocked for 24 hours. An export from a pack is used up only after a file was really saved, shared or printed, so cancelling the share sheet costs nothing. If no video can be shown, for example when you are offline or no ad is available, the app says so and you can try again or use a pack or Premium. Used-up packs cannot be restored. Premium can be restored with Restore purchases on the export sheet or in the Profile tab.

Can I get a refund for a purchase?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple, for example through reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves because we do not receive the payment.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.

Where are my documents stored?

On your device, in the app's own storage. We do not upload them and we cannot see them. A file you export (PDF, Word, Rich Text, plain text or web page) goes where you send it, such as the Files app or the share sheet. If you import an existing resume, the file is read on your device and is not uploaded.

Will an applicant tracking system accept my resume?

Nobody can promise that. Employers use many different systems and each one reads a resume in its own way. The ATS check in CareerKit flags common formatting problems and explains each finding, so use it as a checklist and not as a verdict.

Does it work offline?

Writing, editing and previewing work without a connection. Exporting offline works while you have exports left or Premium. A video and new purchases need a connection. Banner ads only appear when you are online.

What happens if I do not allow tracking?

The app works the same. The ads you see will be less personalised. You can change the choice any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

How do I delete my data?

Open the Profile tab and choose Delete all local data. The app offers to save a backup file first. Deleting the app also removes its data. Files you already exported stay wherever you saved them.
