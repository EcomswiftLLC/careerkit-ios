Support

We reply by email. A message gets answered faster when it includes:

Common questions

The export sheet says no video is available

Tap Export without video to finish that export. Ad availability changes by country and by time of day, and no video can load while you are offline or when your ad privacy choice does not allow ads. You can also tap Try the video again.

I closed the video early and nothing was exported

A video unlocks the export only when it has played to the end. Tap Watch again on the export sheet to start another one.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Open the Profile tab and tap Report an ad, or write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open the Profile tab and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.

I deleted the app and my resumes are gone

Documents are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them, and we cannot recover them. Keep an exported copy of anything important. A full backup of your device may still contain the app's data if you made one before deleting it.

How do I delete all of my data?

Open the Profile tab and choose Delete all local data. We hold no personal data about your use of the app, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Something looks wrong in a template or an exported file

Tell us the template name, the page size you chose (A4 or US Letter), what you expected and what you got. A screenshot of the problem helps. Please do not send a full resume with real personal details unless we ask for it.

I have an idea for a template or a feature

Send it. We read every message, though we cannot promise to build every idea.
