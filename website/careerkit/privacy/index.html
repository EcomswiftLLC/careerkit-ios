Privacy Policy

App: Resume Builder: CareerKit ("CareerKit")

Company: Ecom Swift LLC, 30 N Gould St #37906, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States

Contact: support@ecomswiftllc.com

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what CareerKit does with information. The short version: the things you write in the app stay on your device, and the only outside party that receives any data is the advertising service that pays for the app.

1. The short version

  • CareerKit has no account and no sign-in.
  • Your resumes, cover letters, photos and settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
  • CareerKit is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device and ad data to show and measure ads. You are asked for your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not run our own analytics and we do not sell your resume content.

2. Information that stays on your device

When you use CareerKit you may enter your name, contact details, work history, education, skills, a photo and other text for your resume and cover letters. This content is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to Ecom Swift LLC or to any server we operate, and it is not used to train any model. Nothing in the app sends your document content to an AI service.

If you add a photo, the app reads it through the iOS photo picker, makes a smaller copy and stores that copy inside the resume on your device.

When you export a PDF, print, or create a backup file, the file is created on your device and given to the iOS share sheet, the Files app or the printer you choose. Where it goes after that is your choice. We do not receive it.

3. Information we receive

From the app: none. We do not receive your content, your identity or your usage of the app.

From you, if you write to us: if you email support@ecomswiftllc.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the request. We keep support emails for as long as needed to help you and to meet our legal duties, and then delete them. Please do not send your resume or other sensitive documents unless we ask for them.

From the website: the CareerKit website pages (ecomswiftllc.github.io/careerkit/) are static pages. They do not use cookies of their own, analytics scripts or advertising scripts. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CareerKit is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows adaptive banner ads on browse screens (Home, My Resumes, Templates, Cover Letters and Profile) and one optional rewarded video before you export a finished document. There are no ads in the editor.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use, according to Google:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, and other device and app identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • information about ads you see and interact with
  • device, operating system and app information and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance

We do not receive this data and we do not combine it with anything we hold. Google's use of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and on the page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Some US state privacy laws treat advertising of this kind as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". CareerKit does not sell your content. You can limit ad personalisation as described in the next section.

5. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Consent form (EEA, UK and other regions where required). When you first open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform may appear. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. You can reopen it later in the Profile tab, in the Ad privacy choices row, where one is required.
  • Tracking prompt. After the consent step, iOS may ask whether CareerKit may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared, and ads you see will be less personalised. The app works the same either way.
  • iOS settings. You can change the tracking choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in the same area.
  • Ads cannot be switched off inside the app, because ads are how CareerKit is paid for. If you decline the consent form where one is required, ads may not load for you. In that case no banner appears and exporting a document is not available, because an export needs a completed rewarded video. Writing, editing and previewing still work.

6. Rewarded video and exports

A rewarded video is shown only if you choose to watch it. The reward (one export of the document you are working on) is recorded on your device only. The record contains no personal information and is not sent anywhere.

7. Deleting your data

  • To delete everything CareerKit stores, open the Profile tab in the app and choose Delete all local data. The app offers to save a backup file first, then asks you to confirm. This removes all resumes, cover letters and drafts stored by the app.
  • Deleting the app from your device also removes its stored data.
  • Files you exported or shared (PDFs, backup files) are outside the app. You manage and delete them where you saved or sent them.
  • Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may contain a copy of the app's data according to your Apple settings. That copy is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.
  • We hold no personal data about you from your use of the app, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's controls (https://myadcenter.google.com) or contact Google.

8. Children

CareerKit is a tool for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not designed for the Kids category and does not use child-directed ad treatment. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information from your use of the app, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can write to us at support@ecomswiftllc.com about any request and we will respond within the time the law requires. For data held by Google, exercise your rights with Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Your documents are protected by the security of your device and by iOS app storage protections. We recommend a device passcode. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for the files you export and share.

11. International transfers

Google and its partners may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on legal transfer mechanisms described in Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and where a change is significant we will say so in the app's release notes.
